Our philosophy

A place lives through its people.

Cultures do not preserve themselves. They are carried forward by ordinary people—the baker who wakes before dawn, the winemaker tending vines planted generations ago, the cook keeping family recipes alive, the artisan giving a lifetime to a craft, and the market vendor who knows every producer by name.

We call them the Keepers of Place.

They are not attractions. They are teachers, stewards and hosts—the living expression of a region. They help us understand not only what makes a place distinctive, but how its traditions, values and ways of living continue from one generation to the next.

Every French Detours journey is designed around encounters with them. Because the people of a place are what turn a trip into a journey.

How we travel

Five ways a place reveals itself.

The French Detours Craft brings together five dimensions of l’art de vivre. Together, they create the conditions for curiosity, connection and the wonder of encountering France with fresh eyes.

01
Connection to place

Terroir

Where am I?
Land, climate, history and habit come together to make one village unlike the next. We travel to understand why places become what they are—not simply to collect them.

02
Connection to craft

Savoir-faire

How is this place expressed?
Savoir-faire is the wisdom, patience and care that come from giving yourself to a craft over time. We do not encounter something handmade simply to buy it. We meet the people, hands and generations behind it.

03
Connection to discovery

Curiosité

How do I experience it fully?
We look more closely, ask the next question and leave room for plans to unfold. The goal is not to see more. It is to experience more deeply.

04
Connection to care

L’accueil

How am I welcomed?
L’accueil is the quiet art of noticing what someone needs before they realize they need it. French Detours practices hospitality as attention: thoughtful pacing, calm preparation and the small details that allow a guest to feel expected and at ease.

05
Connection to others

La convivialité

Who do I become connected to?
There are no manufactured moments—only shared meals, conversation, laughter and time together. We travel not simply alongside one another, but return home carrying one another’s stories.

Meet Traci · Founder and host

A personal way
into France.

French Detours grew from years of returning to France and learning that the most memorable experiences rarely begin with a list of sights. They begin with a person, a conversation or an invitation to look more closely.

Traci personally develops each journey around the people and places that give a region its character. She meets the hosts, winemakers, cooks, makers and guides before introducing them to her travelers, choosing depth over volume and relationships over itineraries.

She also travels with every group—holding the details, noticing individual needs and adjusting the pace when the moment calls for it. From the first conversation through the return home, the experience remains personal.
