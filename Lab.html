Questions

Good things to ask before you start.

Do I need to know anything about AI?+

No. Discovery starts with how your business works, not with the technology. You learn the tools by using them on your own work.

What tools do you build with?+

Whatever fits how you already work. We favor tools you already pay for and add new ones only when they earn their place.

What does it cost?+

Every engagement is scoped in discovery, so you know the plan before phase 1 begins. Start with a conversation and we will talk it through.

Who owns what we build?+

You do. Everything is set up in your accounts, documented in your playbook, and yours to keep. No lock-in.

What if I am short on time?+

Choose the weekly review. We build, you make the calls, and a teammate can take the wheel in phase 2.
