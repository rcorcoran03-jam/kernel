The 12-week partnership

We drive, then you drive.

One build in two parts. Phase 1 lays the foundation while you watch it being made. Phase 2 hands you the wheel to customize and connect it, with us beside you.

Before0102 · Phase 103 · Phase 204

  1. Before we begin

    A free conversation

    We talk through what you want room for and whether Kernel Lab fits. No commitment, and it doesn’t count toward the 8 hours.

  2. Week 1 · about 8 hours of your time

    Discover and scope

    Two focused working sessions to map how a real week runs and record a baseline of where the hours go.

    You leave with a written plan and your week-one baseline

  3. Weeks 2–7 · we drive

    See one: build the foundation

    We build inside the tools you already use while you work alongside us. Your outcomes dashboard goes live with it.

    You leave with phase 1 tools, live and ready to customize

  4. Weeks 8–11 · you drive

    Do one: customize and integrate

    Your team leads the sessions, customizing what we built and connecting it to the rest of your tools. We coach and review.

    You leave with customizations and integrations live

  5. Week 12

    Teach one: hand off and measure

    We review results against your baseline, and you teach the playbook forward to a teammate.

    You leave with a playbook you’ve used and a results review

Two ways to take part

Dedicated co-working sessions

Build alongside us in live sessions each week. The fastest way to own the method.

Just the weekly review

We build; you join one weekly review to make the calls. A teammate can take the wheel in phase 2.

What you own

When we leave, everything stays.

No lock-in, and nothing hidden in our accounts. The point of Teach one is that you can keep building without us.

  • Tools in your accounts

    Everything is set up under your logins, in the apps you already pay for. You hold the keys.

  • A working playbook

    How each tool works, why it was built that way, and how to change it. Written as we go, and used before hand-off.

  • A practiced method

    Your team has already run phase 2 and taught it forward, so the skill is proven, not promised.

  • Room to keep building

    A list of what to build next, and the confidence to start it. The hours you get back go to your best ideas.

Questions

Good things to ask before you start.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Discovery starts with how your business works, not with the technology. You learn the tools by using them on your own work.

Is the first conversation part of the 8 hours?

No. The first conversation is free and separate. The roughly 8 hours are the two working sessions in week one, once you decide to begin.

What tools do you build with?

Whatever fits how you already work. We favor tools you already pay for and add new ones only when they earn their place.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped in discovery, so you see the plan before phase 1 begins. Start a conversation and we’ll talk it through.

What if I’m short on time?

Choose the weekly review. We build, you make the calls, and a teammate can take the wheel in phase 2.

When will the Opportunity Scan be live?

We’re building it now. Until then, send your answers with an inquiry and we’ll use them to prepare for our first conversation.
