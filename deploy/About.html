Meet Kate

Kate Corcoran helps leaders see what is essential.

For more than 16 years, Kate has worked at the intersection of enterprise strategy, human insight, healthcare, and growth. She has led complex research and market-development programs, advised senior executives through consequential decisions, and translated competing sources of information into clear strategic direction.

Her work begins beneath the presenting problem — with the identity, purpose, relationships, and human needs that give the work meaning. Her experience in healthcare strategy and end-of-life care has shaped a central conviction: clarity is not the same as certainty. It is knowing what matters enough to orient around, even when the future cannot be predicted.

At Kernel, Kate brings particular depth to clarity, identity, and strategic orientation.

Meet Ryan

Ryan Corcoran helps organizations put clarity into motion.

Ryan's career spans product strategy, digital experience, brand positioning, analytics, commercialization, and creative production. He has brought clinical, technical, commercial, and creative teams together to turn complex needs into products, experiences, and operating practices people can actually use.

His foundation in biomedical science, experience teaching yoga, and career connecting strategy with execution have given him an embodied understanding of how living systems adapt: through feedback, repetition, relationship, and continual adjustment.

At Kernel, Ryan brings particular depth to practice, systems, and bringing strategy to life.
