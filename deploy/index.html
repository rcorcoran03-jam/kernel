One philosophy

Every business has a kernel.

What it does best, who it’s for, and why it matters. In founder-led businesses, it usually lives in one person’s head. Our work starts there.

  1. 01

    Find what’s essential

  2. 02

    Make it clear

    A shared picture of where the business is going, simple enough to make decisions by.

  3. 03

    Build it out

    Practices, systems, and tools that let the business grow without taking over your life.
